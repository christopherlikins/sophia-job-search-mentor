# Sophia: Job Search Mentor

A job-search and career-strategy mentor for Claude, built around my own approach to finding work in Denmark. It is a method with light coaching, not a copy generator: Sophia asks questions, offers options, critiques them and sets small exercises, so the words you end up with are yours.

**This is not authoritative.** It is one approach, based on my own experience, and it overlaps with other methods. Use it as one resource among many. I would like feedback, and challenge is welcome.

## What it helps with

- **Positioning:** LinkedIn bio, CV headline, elevator pitch
- **Messaging:** outreach and follow-ups that sound like you
- **Networking:** relationship-first approaches
- **Interview prep:** STAR stories and practice
- **Market targeting:** reading job postings and deciding where to aim

It is calibrated for Copenhagen and the Danish market. Parts of it may carry over to other job markets, but I have not tested that.

## How it works

Sophia works in a loop: generate options, critique them, ask reflective questions, suggest an exercise, then refine based on your answers. The Copenhagen calibration (relationship-first, quietly confident, in your own voice) is my reading of the market from experience. Treat it as a point of view, not a fact.

## Install (Claude)

1. Download `sophia-job-search-mentor.zip` from the Releases page, or from the repository files.
2. In Claude, open **Customize > Skills** and upload the zip. Menu names vary by plan and app version. If you do not see Skills, look for **Plugins > Add > Upload plugin**.
3. Start a chat and say something like "Sophia, help me sharpen my LinkedIn headline for a [role] search in Copenhagen."

Sophia is packaged as an Agent Skill, an open format that other tools also support. I have only tested it in Claude.

## Privacy

Whatever you paste goes to the AI tool you are using. Do not share CPR numbers, confidential employer material or anything else you would not want processed there.

## How it was made

I built this with AI help, first as a custom GPT and later ported to a Claude skill. The method and framing come from my own job-search experience. Vionis, the loop framework underneath, is an early experiment of mine in building a cognitive framework.
